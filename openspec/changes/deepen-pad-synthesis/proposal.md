## Why

Glass Tide combines a wavetable, FM and grains, but all lanes share one amplitude contour. Most of its initial evolution comes from a 12-second ramp. After that ramp, a held chord depends heavily on performer movement. A flagship Blue pad should give a player a reason to stay on one chord, while leaving enough tonal stability to recognize a chord change.

## What Changes

- Supply Tideline as a playable sound-design study for the existing instrument, alongside a loudness-matched comparison with Glass Tide.
- Propose independently timed modulation contours, richer granular and spectral sources, and a stereo feedback instrument for the next engine iteration.
- Qualify each addition with dry and wet musical examples, listening evidence and the existing cross-target regression and performance gates.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `evolving-pads`: Long-form polyphonic pads with independent internal motion and playable transitions.

## Impact

The study is an optional external pack for the existing C/Lua hosts. Future work affects the compiled patch layout, voice lifetime, modulation API, resource preparation, editors and automated render suites. Existing v1 packs must retain their original sound on the revised engine.
